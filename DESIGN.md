## Brand & Style

### Narrative & Brand Personality
The brand reflects an esteemed heritage of artisanal silversmithing and fine bullion curation, balancing generational craftsmanship with contemporary atelier elegance. The digital experience conveys prestige, absolute authenticity, architectural discipline, and understated opulence.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning collectors of bespoke sterling silver art, high-net-worth investors monitoring physical bullion (gold, silver, platinum), and bridal/gift connoisseurs seeking enduring luxury.
- **Emotional Intent:** Instill confidence, serene sophistication, tactile reverence, and financial security through measured proportions, quiet transitions, and uncompromised clarity.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic:** Editorial Minimal Luxury with Subtle Classical Skeuomorphism.
- **Key Characteristics:** Generous alabaster whitespace, razor-thin hairline borders in antique gold, luminous metallic accents, crisp geometric numerical precision, and tactile card surfaces evocative of fine velvet presentation cases and polished marble display plinths.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
The primary surface strategy relies on a radiant light foundational canvas offset by deep obsidian contrast points to establish museum-grade balance:

- **Primary (`#111111` - Deep Obsidian Black):** Anchors high-impact editorial headings, primary call-to-action buttons, high-value bullion value totals, and primary navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#C6A66B` - Royal Antique Gold):** Applied selectively to fine trim borders, status highlights, live bullion market indicators, active tab underlines, and verified assay seal badges.
- **Tertiary (`#1C1A17` - Rich Charcoal):** Used for elevated cards, modal footers, secondary actions, and contrasting table headers in administrative modules.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F6F2` - Alabaster Warm Off-White):** Forms the foundational background across editorial layouts and product showcases, mitigating clinical glare while honoring photographic gemstone fidelity.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - `#FFFFFF` (Pure White): Reserved for surface container cards, data sheet interiors, and elevated overlays.
  - `#E8E8E8` (Soft Border Gray): Serves as the primary neutral divider and container outline to sustain structural discipline without visual friction.
  - Live Ticker Status: `#2B6B48` (Muted Malachite Green for market gains) and `#8B2626` (Heritage Garnet Red for market dips).

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy & Pairings
- **Headlines & Narrative (Playfair Display):** Employs high-contrast serifs with sculpted terminals to evoke fine engraving, heritage hallmarks, and timeless luxury print. Used for collection names, hero announcements, and philosophical quotes.
- **Interface & Data Architecture (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean optical geometric geometry. Neutral, legible, and balanced across high-density inventory sheets, assay certificates, live spot pricing, and technical grammage specifications.
- **Letter Spacing & Micro-Typography:** All uppercase category labels, hallmarks, metal fineness chips (e.g., `925 AYAR GÜMÜŞ`), and table column keys mandate wide tracking (`0.12em` to `0.18em`) with heavy font weight (`700`) to communicate deliberate architectural refinement.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy & Proportions
The design system deploys a classic 12-column layout on desktop screens (max-width `1440px`), collapsing gracefully into an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports. Layouts prioritize generous framing, preserving empty negative space as a luxury commodity.

### Adaptability & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`. Product galleries leverage uneven editorial spans (e.g., 7 columns hero visual / 5 columns transactional panel).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Product grids adapt strictly to 2-up configurations to maintain high visual scale.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`. Complex bullion tables transform into horizontally scrollable precision cards or swipeable compact ticker modules.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification & Subtle Refinement
Visual elevation avoids harsh, unrefined drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and hairline structural borders.

- **Base Canvas:** Flat Alabaster (`#F7F6F2`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Plinths):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px structural outline of `#E8E8E8`. Layered with an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Carts, Popovers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a dual-line stroke illusion: `1px solid rgba(198, 166, 107, 0.35)` combined with `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Overlays, Diamond Loupe Modals):** Crisp backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a veiled Obsidian backdrop `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.72)`, suspending the centered white asset container with an ethereal gold glow `box-shadow: 0 24px 60px -12px rgba(198, 166, 107, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

### Edge Architecture
The design system mandates a strict `0` (Sharp) corner language across primary interactive elements, mirrors the geometry of cut gemstones, precision bullion ingots, and architectural showcase glass.

- **Buttons, Panels, & Cards:** Strictly `0px` radius. Sharp, diamond-cut edges reinforce seriousness, architectural discipline, and structural permanence.
- **Micro Chips & Market Trend Pills:** Subtle exception permitted: hairline square badges or minimal geometric chamfers.
- **Dividers:** Crisp 1px strokes running horizontally or vertically, never exceeding 1px to emulate jeweler wire.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Atelier Button):** Sharp-edged, background Obsidian Black (`#111111`), text Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), styled in uppercase `label-caps`. Hover activates an inner inset gold hairline border (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #C6A66B`) with smooth color transition.
- **Secondary Action (Gold Inset):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #C6A66B`, text `#111111`. Hover fills the background with `#C6A66B` and transitions text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Link Button:** Underlined with a 1px antique gold rule positioned 4px beneath the baseline, expanding outward from center on hover.

### Luxury Product Cards
- Encased in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px perimeter of `#E8E8E8`.
- Upper region contains an uncompromised square or 4:5 aspect ratio image against an alabaster studio backdrop.
- Subtle badge anchor in the upper-left corner designating fineness (`925 STERLING`, `24K HAS`).
- Hover state induces a smooth 1.03x optical image zoom and reveals an instant "Hızlı İncele" (Quick View) action bar along the lower image axis.
- Card footer cleanly delineates title in `headline-sm` with price rendered in `price-numeric`.

### Live Bullion Market Ticker
- High-density status strip placed at the global masthead or dedicated ticker component.
- Dark theme inversion: Obsidian Black (`#111111`) base, framed by `#C6A66B` bottom rule.
- Live metal values (Gold/Gram, Silver/Ounce, Platinum/Gram) display metal designation in muted gold uppercase labels, paired with a green or red directional arrow and numeric shift values.
- Real-time refresh pulse signaled via a minute radial dot glowing momentarily in `#C6A66B`.

### Administrative & Inventory Tables
- Crisp, spreadsheet-caliber data visualization for vault inventory and order fulfillment.
- Header row in `#1C1A17` with bold, uppercase tracked typography in `#FFFFFF`.
- Data rows alternate subtly between `#FFFFFF` and `#F7F6F2`.
- Border-bottom pinned to a hairline `1px solid #E8E8E8`.
- Gram weight, karat calculation, and spot margin delta formatted in tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #E8E8E8`, sharp corners (`0px`).
- Inactive state utilizes muted charcoal placeholders.
- Focus state switches border instantly to `1px solid #C6A66B` accompanied by a gentle ambient halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 166, 107, 0.15)`.

### Radios & Checkboxes
- Square geometries with zero border-radius.
- Unchecked: `1px solid #C6A66B` border over pure white background.
- Checked: Obsidian Black (`#111111`) fill with a centered micro-square accent in Royal Antique Gold (`#C6A66B`).
